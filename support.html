Support

Most questions are answered below. If not, send us a message.

Questions

How do you test and grade parts?

Graphics cards run a three-hour stress test while we log temperatures. Memory gets four passes of MemTest86, drives are checked with SMART tools, and CPUs and boards are booted in a test bench. The grade (A, B or C) only describes cosmetic condition; every grade passes the same tests.

What does the 3-month warranty cover?

If a part from our shop stops working within three months for any reason other than physical damage, we repair, replace or refund it. Classifieds ads are between you and the seller and are not covered.

How long does delivery take?

Two to three working days across Gauteng and four to six working days elsewhere in South Africa. You can also collect from our Johannesburg test bench.

Can I return a part I don't need?

Yes, within seven days if it's in the condition we sent it. Contact us with your order number to start a return.

How do I sell on Classifieds?

Create a free account, then choose Post an ad on the Classifieds page. Buyers message you through ComputiX, and you can remove the ad from your profile once it's sold.

Will you buy my parts outright?

Often, yes. Send us the model and condition using the form and we'll reply with an offer, usually within a day.
